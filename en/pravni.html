Licence and terms

ČlovíčekHD is not on sale yet. The full terms and conditions and the end-user licence agreement (EULA) will be published no later than the start of sales.

Licence model

A licence is sold per device and per specific version of the game. The Home licence (€17 / CZK 330) is for one individual for private, non-commercial use. The Commercial licence (€170 / CZK 3,300) is for businesses, self-employed people, schools, institutions and any professional or commercial use. A new major version of the game may require a new licence.

Consumer rights

Consumers generally have the right to withdraw from a distance contract within 14 days. For digital content (a licence key), this right ends if delivery begins with the consumer's express prior consent and the consumer acknowledges losing the right of withdrawal (Section 1837(l) of the Czech Civil Code). We will ask for this consent with a separate checkbox at purchase. Your rights regarding defective performance remain unaffected.

Paying with bitcoin

Prices will be set in euros and Czech crowns; bitcoin is only the payment method. BTC payments cannot be reversed and the network fee is paid by the buyer. We record received payments in Czech crowns at the exchange rate on the day of receipt.

Out-of-court dispute resolution

The Czech Trade Inspection Authority (www.coi.cz) handles out-of-court resolution of consumer disputes.

Privacy

This website

This is a static website that collects no personal data. It has no forms, user accounts, analytics or advertising tools, and loads nothing from third-party servers.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security reasons. Transfers of data to the USA are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for details.

Purchases (once sales begin)

To deliver your licence key we will process your email address, licence type and transaction ID (txid). The legal basis is performance of the contract and compliance with accounting and tax obligations. We keep the data for the period required by accounting and tax law.

Children

The game collects no personal data from players and saves progress only on the device. Purchases should be made by a parent or another adult.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict processing, to data portability and to object. You can lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz). The controller is the operator named above.

Cookies

This website uses no cookies and stores nothing in your browser, which is why there is no consent banner. If we ever want to measure traffic, we will only do so after you give your consent.
