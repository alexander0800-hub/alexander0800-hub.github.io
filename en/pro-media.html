Copy-ready description

A family Stone Age platformer about Človíček, who crosses an entire era to find the one he loves. 100 levels, a dino buddy, no ads and no microtransactions.

ČlovíčekHD is a family 2D platformer set in the Stone Age. At the Festival of the First Fire, Človíček meant to ask Človíčková to marry him, but at midnight she was carried off and the village fire went out. Players cross 100 levels in ten hand-painted lands, gather friends along the way – including Dráček the baby dino – and find out who is really behind the kidnapping. The game has a full Czech voice-over (text and voice-over in more languages are in preparation), plays offline, and contains no ads or in-game purchases.
