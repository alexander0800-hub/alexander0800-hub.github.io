Story

The Festival of the First Fire ended in a kidnapping

By the campfire, Grandpa Pazourek tells the tale of Človíček. At the Festival of the First Fire, he meant to ask Miss Človíčková to marry him. But at midnight the Frowners carried her off, and the village fire went out.

Človíček sets off across the entire Stone Age to find her. Along the way he lights signal fires, meets friends you will love and discovers who is really behind the kidnapping.

“Some enemies are simply lost.”

Characters

  • ČlovíčekA brave hero with a club. His only fear is arriving too late.
  • ČlovíčkováA redhead with a bone hairpin who leaves him messages along the way.
  • DráčekA little green dino. He bites, sniffs and wags his tail when you pat him.
  • ChlupáčA mammoth with big ears, big eyes and an even bigger heart.

FAQ

How long does a licence last?

A Home licence is permanent for the purchased version of the game. A Commercial licence runs for 5 years from the day the key is issued.

What happens after five years?

The game switches to demo mode and your saves stay untouched. We warn you 60, 30 and 7 days before the licence expires. Renewing for another 5 years costs 50% of the licence price and comes with a new key.

Can I move the game to another computer?

A licence covers one device. When you replace your computer, write to us and we will issue a key for the new one; the old key stops working.

Does the game need the internet?

No. The game runs offline, the key is verified on the device and your progress is stored only on your machine.

How much is a new major version?

If you own the previous major version, you can buy the new one for half the regular price.
