Two Fonts Bundle — Hranol & Kulatina

A bundle of two AlexanderSoft typeface families, sold as one product: Hranol for modern UI and headings, Kulatina for kids' games and friendly interfaces. Six styles in total, full Central European diacritics.

Hranol

A modern geometric grotesque for headings and UI in games and on the web.

Rounded soft sans · Light / Regular / Bold · version 1.005

Kulatina
